- **Reviewer:** independent QA agent, separate from the drafting context. **Revision run (2026-11-16):** the drafting agent revised the spec and survey against this record; every finding was fixed, and the `Disposition` column below records the section changed.
- **Branch:** `plan/m13-internal-variants` (base `main` @ `8394f1f`; QA commit on top of `3ee5f05`).

- **Method:** independent GLB parse of every committed asset (JSON chunk + accessors; triangle counts, bounds, materials, node transforms); direct grep of `src/renderer/engineScene.ts`, `partVariants.ts`, `sectionCaps.ts`, `partLoader.ts`, `referenceFrames.ts`, `src/catalog/**`, `catalog/*.json`; `npm test` (`104/104`, `21` files) and `npm run typecheck` (both pass); manual arithmetic from the spec's own formulas; inspection of the committed `RF-10.png` / `RF-6.png`; and sampling of the survey's load-bearing web sources. **No WebGL/headless browser** is available in this VM, so benchmark calls/submitted/VRAM/p95 and the visual checklist were not re-executed; those rows are checked against the committed M10/M11/M12 records and the static arithmetic, and that limitation is stated where it applies.
- **Result:** **21 findings — 2 blockers, 12 should-fix, 7 nits.** The scene baselines, rod/stock triangle counts, x8 multiplication, with-cover totals and the **319,128 / 872** cap guard all reproduce arithmetically; two integration tests do not. The decisive failure is **QA-01**: the RF-22 proof frame is pinned at `X = 402.5 mm`, which is the centre of the **0.724 mm inter-rod gap**, so no rod is cut and the claimed rod section cannot exist. The second blocker is **QA-02**: the combined piston+rod triangle accounting is not actually wired into the existing piston swap.

**Revision result:** **2 blockers fixed, 12 should-fixes fixed, 7 nits fixed; 0 rebutted.** The `Disposition` column records the spec/survey section changed for each finding.
