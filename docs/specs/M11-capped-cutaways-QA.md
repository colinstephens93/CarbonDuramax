## Claims I could not verify

- `STENCIL_BITS = 0` on the target GPU and the live 96/579,484 / 73/542,384 measurements: I confirmed the constructor has no `stencil` and reproduced the numbers from `.m11probe/out/captures.json` and `docs/M10-MEASUREMENTS.md`, but did not re-run headless Chrome.
- The shader-fill GPU timings (+2.7–3.2 ms), the first-activation compile delta (273 vs 279 ms) and the five-state percentages 14.4/42.3/13.1 %: the shader prototype is not committed; only quoted numbers exist, and the committed pixel-diff script gives 4.23 % on the RF-6 pair (S9).
- The `?benchmark=1` frame p95 / `avgCpuRenderMs` rows and the "0 differing pixels" section-off invariance: build-time measurements.
- The `DoubleSide` claim for all internal and exterior materials: docs assert it; I did not parse the glTF material flags.
- The 16,000 cap-triangle ceiling in the *worst-case* states (a plane through a spring row or the bank): only X=402.5 (609 shell tris) is measured; spring hull caps (~1 k tris) and Y-plane caps are unmeasured.
- The RF-14 and RF-17 camera poses: no prototype capture uses them (the prototype's piston candidates used `[0.62,−0.34,0.38]`/`[0.58,−0.28,0.30]`, and its Y state used Y = −185 mm with camera `[0.25,0.75,0.55]`), so their framing is asserted, not validated.

## Bottom line — fix before the owner sees this

1. **B1 — the non-watertight list is wrong (5, not 6; `ext_front_cover_1` is watertight).** Test 5(d) and §4.3/§14.6.9 cannot be satisfied; fix the list and re-pin.

Decide per-part slicing vs named dropped caps, and fix A2.
3. **B3 — RF-17 cannot pass its checklist:** with Exterior On, the Y=0 shell cap (0.247 m², covering the crank axis) hides the crankshaft and RH bank. Use Exterior Off / X-Ray or re-scope the frame.
