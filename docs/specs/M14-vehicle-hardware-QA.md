# M14 Stock Vehicle-Side Hardware — Independent QA Record (D-037 step 4)

- **Reviewer:** independent QA agent, separate from the drafting context. The spec and survey were **not edited** in this run.
- **Branch:** `plan/m14-vehicle-hardware` (base `main` @ `002b2c2`; QA commit on top of `5372708`).
- **Reviewed:** [`docs/specs/M14-vehicle-hardware.md`](./M14-vehicle-hardware.md) (1,125 lines at QA time; 1,213 after the revision) and [`docs/research/M14-vehicle-hardware-survey.md`](../research/M14-vehicle-hardware-survey.md) (317 lines at QA time; 339 after the revision).
- **Method:** independent binary-GLB parse of the M10 shell, the ten stock GLBs, the five M8 variants, the M13 forged rod and the M12 cover; direct grep of `src/renderer/engineScene.ts`, `sectionCaps.ts`, `intakeCover.ts`, `variantLoader.ts`, `partVariants.ts`, `viewLayout.ts`, `src/ui/referenceFrames.ts`, `src/main.ts`, `src/ui/engineControls.ts`, the existing test suite and the committed M8/M10/M11/M12/M13 records; `npx vitest run src` (`114/114`, 23 files) and arithmetic from the spec's own formulas. **No WebGL/headless browser** is available in this VM, so benchmark calls/submitted/VRAM/p95 and the reference-frame renders were not re-executed; those values are checked against the committed records and the static arithmetic. **No Blender** is available, so the hardware asset is a design contract, not a measured asset. **The owner's reference photos are not present in `~/projects` or the workspace**, so the photo citations could not be inspected directly; that is recorded as a finding, not silently accepted.
- **Result:** **27 findings — 4 blockers, 14 should-fix, 9 nits.** The central budget arithmetic (310,900 / 319,128 / 872, 31,400, 70,364, 81,164, 350,528, 328,228) reproduces exactly. The decisive failures are geometric and testability failures: **QA-01** (the frozen V2 high-flow intake passes through the proposed stock radiator and intercooler, so the OD-2 yield rules cannot produce an overlap-free selectable combination), **QA-02/QA-03** (the airbox collar and the exhaust muffler cannot satisfy the §7.4 bounds that test 3 pins within ±0.10 mm), and **QA-04** (the mutual-exclusion test is a tautology over the pure resolver and never checks the rendered scene, so the 350,528-triangle forbidden state is not actually tested).
- **Single most important finding:** **QA-01** — the `intake_highflow` + Vehicle combination is selectable, hides only the airbox, and puts V2's frozen intake tract through the stock radiator and intercooler envelopes. No test, frame or acceptance step renders that combination.
