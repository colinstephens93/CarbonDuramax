# M8 Variant References — Published Visual Anchors for Five Aftermarket LLY Parts

Purpose: gather **external published data** that can honestly anchor the *visual* 3D models of the five
aftermarket variants named in `docs/specs/M6-parts-catalog.md` §2.2 — `turbo_large`, `intake_highflow`,
`exhaust_4in`, `intercooler_upgraded`, `pistons_lowcomp`. Researched 2026-11-14 from public web pages.
Nothing here was measured by us and nothing was interpolated: values appear as published with their
original units, arithmetic on published inputs is labeled DERIVED, and marketing percentages/power
claims are recorded only where they describe physical form or provenance, flagged grade D. All sources
are LB7/LLY/LBZ/LMM-family; no L5P data was imported. Stock references already fixed by the L7 survey
(turbo wheels §11, piston CH/pin/bowl §8) are cited, not re-derived.

**Reliability grades (L7 convention)**
- **A** — GM service literature / GM-published data
- **B** — Established diesel publication, or an OE-parts manufacturer publishing OE specs
- **C** — Specialist vendor spec page (a seller publishing dimensions its part must fit/build to), CARB
  executive-order filings, OE-replacement makers' catalog data
- **D** — Forum post, marketplace listing, or marketing copy (unverified)

**Verdicts:** VERIFIED-PUBLISHED / PARTIALLY PUBLISHED / NOT PUBLICLY AVAILABLE.

## 1. Large variable-geometry turbo upgrade (`turbo_large`)

- **Baseline already published (L7 §11):** stock Garrett GT3788VA — compressor **62.8 mm inducer / 88 mm exducer**, 11 blades; turbine **71.3 mm inducer / 65.7 mm exducer**, 13 blades; vane height **14.4 mm** (DuramaxTuner) vs **15 mm** (DrivingLine/XDP). Grade C/B. No fixed A/R exists (it is a VGT).
- **The key honesty finding:** every drop-in VGT vendor publishes a **single leading wheel diameter** for each wheel, not an inducer/exducer pair. The numbers below are leading diameters as the vendor states them.
- Calibrated Power **Stealth STR 61/67** (CPS200130, drop-in, 2004.5–2010 LLY/LBZ/LMM): **61 mm** 7+7-blade extended-tip billet **2618** compressor wheel; **66.5 mm** 10-blade **713C nickel-alloy** turbine wheel; **11 mm tall turbine vanes**; airflow "**67 lb/min vs 59 lb/min stock**"; "no tuning required"; 650 RWHP (grade D claim). [wcfab.com](https://www.wcfab.com/i-31645784-2004-5-2010-lly-lbz-lmm-duramaxstealth-str-61-67-drop-in-vvt-turbo.html) — vendor spec page, **grade C**.

[wcfab.com](https://www.wcfab.com/i-30158460-2004-5-2010-lly-lbz-lmm-duramaxstealth-mach-1-63-67-drop-in-vvt-turbo.html) — **grade C** (power claim D).

[wcfab.com](https://www.wcfab.com/i-31644655-2004-5-2010-lly-lbz-lmm-duramax-fleece-63mm-drop-in-vvt-cheetah-turbo.html) — **grade C** (boost/power claims D).
- **BD Diesel Screamer** (1045840, 2004.5–2016 LLY/LBZ/LMM): **65 mm** billet compressor wheel; "mixed flow" larger turbine wheel; "up to **85 lb/min** of airflow"; suggested max **690 crank HP**; drop-in, stock-appearing. [lethalperformance.com](https://www.lethalperformance.com/products/bd-diesel-bd-duramax-screamer-turbo-chevy-2004-5-2010-lly-lbz-lmm-1045840-custom) — **grade C** (hp figure D).
- **Industrial Injection XR Series** (2004.5–2010): **61 mm** billet compressor wheel in a "new Garrett modified turbocharger", polished compressor cover; listing states "**LLY Requires 771864-0001 Adapter**". [industrialinjection.com](https://industrialinjection.com/products/2004-5-2010-6-6l-duramax-xr-series-turbocharger-61mm). XR2 version: **65 mm** billet wheel, polished housing. - **JZ Manufacturing 68 mm "drop-in" R-vane** (LLY/LBZ/LMM): built from a new Garrett 4094 Powermax Stage 2; **68 mm** 6×6 billet compressor; 10-blade **68 mm** turbine; proprietary "R" vane; "supports up to 775 HP". [pistontwistin.org listing](https://pistontwistin.org/products/68mm-r-vane-drop-in-vgt-turbocharger-lly-lbz-lmm-duramax-jz-manufacturing) — **grade C** (hp claim D).
- **DDP 68 mm** (on Pure Diesel Power's 2004.5–2005 LLY turbo page): "rated for **750 hp+**", built from a brand-new turbo. [puredieselpower.com](https://puredieselpower.com/chevy-gm-products/2004.5-2005-gm-6.6l-lly-replacement-turbos) — **grade D** (marketplace/marketing).
- **Advertised power range across the family: ~600–775 RWHP (grade D marketing).** These figures are not comparable to the M6 model's 385.9 hp full build; record as marketing context only.
- **Does the upgrade reuse the stock VGT housing?** Published coverage is split: Fleece explicitly reuses stock housings; Calibrated Power/BD/Industrial Injection/JZ sell complete replacement drop-in VGT units that bolt in place of the stock charger (Calibrated Power lists the whole OEM supersession list incl. LLY 736554-series part numbers and offers VGT exhaust-housing finish options, implying a new housing is included). **Verdict: PARTIALLY PUBLISHED.**
- **Vendor-checklist coverage:** PPE (Pacific Performance Engineering) publishes no LLY VGT drop-in upgrade in the sources located — its LLY turbo-adjacent parts are the Zilla turbo inlet, manifolds/up-pipes and outlet adapters. **Danville Performance no longer trades** (multiple forum notices, grade D), so its popular 63.5/65/68 mm drop-ins have no current vendor spec page; a Calibrated Power blog still indexes "[3794 by Danville Performance](https://blog.duramaxtuner.com/blog/drop-in-duramax-turbo-options)" for 2004.5–2010 without dimensions. XDP re-sells these vendors' units rather than publishing its own specs.
- **NOT PUBLICLY AVAILABLE:** upgrade compressor *exducer* diameters, turbine *inducer/exducer pair* (only one ~66–66.5 mm number), compressor-cover outside diameter, any dimensioned drawing, and any fixed A/R (none exists for a VGT). The stock-vane 14.4–15 mm vs aftermarket "11 mm tall vanes" claims are **unreconciled** — do not silently reconcile them.
- **Verdict for the section: PARTIALLY PUBLISHED** — class/leading wheel diameters and the housing strategy are published; the full wheel/housing envelope is not.

## 2. 4-inch exhaust system (`exhaust_4in`)

- **4 in = 101.6 mm** — definitional (DERIVED conversion). VERIFIED by definition.
- **Stock LLY exhaust: 3.5 in (88.9 mm) turbo-back, aluminized, with catalytic converter and muffler.** DieselPowerProducts' 2004.5–05 LLY exhaust guide: "came factory equipped with a 3.5" turbo back aluminized exhaust with a catalytic converter and muffler"; BD Power's exhaust-brake fitment notes "typical factory exhaust size of 3.5" on 2001-2007 LB7/LLY/LBZ". Grade **B/C**. Two sources agree; forum posts corroborate (grade D). [dieselpowerproducts.com](https://dieselpowerproducts.com/collections/c-286-20045-05-66l-lly-gm-duramax-exhaust-kits), [xtremediesel.com](https://www.xtremediesel.com/bd-power-duramax-exhaust-brake).
- **Stock turbo downpipe is 3.5" but "more a flattened out exhaust pipe"** — a crushed oval section for clearance; PPE's 3" replacement describes "the factory LLY/LBZ/LMM downpipe uses a restrictive crushed oval design right after the turbine outlet". Grade **C** (vendor description), grade D (forum). [dieselpowerproducts.com LB7 guide](https://dieselpowerproducts.com/collections/c-267-2001-04-66l-lb7-gm-duramax-exhaust-components?pagenum=4), [fullforcediesel.com PPE 117000500](https://www.fullforcediesel.com/ppe-downpipe-117000500).
- **Kit types sold for the 2004.5–05 LLY (all 4-inch main tubing):**
  - **MBRP S6000AL** — 4-inch **cat-back**, single side exit, "street profile", **aluminized steel**, **T304 stainless tip**, listed 2001–2005 Ext/Crew Cab. [mbrp.com](https://www.mbrp.com/products/s6000al) — grade C.
  - **MBRP Armor Plus 4" "Turbo Direct Pipe Back"** — complete downpipe-back kit in **T409 stainless**; the page fetched lists 2001–2004 LB7 fitment, so LLY-specific T409 coverage remains PARTIAL. [dieselpowerproducts.com](https://dieselpowerproducts.com/products/p-11959-mbrp-pro-series-4-downpipe-back-single-side-exit-t409-stainless-steel-exhaust-system-01-07-66l-gm-duramax-aspx) — grade C.
  - **Diamond Eye K4120A** — 4-inch **cat-back** single outlet for 2004–2005 LLY, **aluminized**, and **"INCLUDES TURBO DIRECT PIPE 321056"**, which is itself a **3-inch** downpipe. [lmperformance.com](https://www.lmperformance.com/1293637/k4120a-diamond-eye-kit-4in-cbsgl-w-tdp-al-04-05-chevy-gmc-66l-duramax-2500-3500.html), [321056 listing](https://fidanza.com/diamond-eye-turbo-direct-3in-al-chevy-gmc-lly-lbz-llm-duramax-04-10-321056/) — grade C.
  - **Flo-Pro FPR 820** — 4-inch **cat-back**, **aluminized steel**, includes a high-flow muffler, "100% mandrel-bent", listed 2001–2007. [shoprpmoutlet.com](https://www.shoprpmoutlet.com/fpr-820-flo-pro-4-inch-aluminized-cat-back-single-exit-exhaust-01-07-gm-duramax-6-6-l.htm) — grade C.
  - **Visual consequence:** packaged "4-inch" kits do **not** reach the turbo at 4 inches — a ~3-inch round downpipe replaces the crushed 3.5" stocker and the 4" section starts at/after the cat (Diamond Eye literally sells the DP as 3"; MBRP sells a separate 3" turbo down pipe, GM8424). **Verdict: PARTIALLY PUBLISHED.**
- **Materials:** aluminized steel (value line), **T409 stainless** (premium line), **T304** for tips; the choice is published per kit. Grade **C**.
- **Pipe wall thickness: "16-gauge"** for MBRP 4" T409 and aluminized systems (reseller copy). 16-gauge sheet = **1.52 mm nominal** (DERIVED from gauge table). Single reseller source; no manufacturer page found stating wall thickness — **grade C/D, low confidence**.
- **Mufflers (published dimensions):** Diamond Eye 4" universal mufflers — **4" center inlet/outlet, 24" body, 30" overall**, round, in aluminized **louvered** (460005) or **409 SS perforated** (460031) core. [carid 460005](https://www.carid.com/diamond-eye-performance/aluminized-steel-round-louvered-gray-exhaust-muffler-mpn-460005.html), [carid 460031](https://www.carid.com/diamond-eye-performance/409-ss-round-performance-perforated-gray-exhaust-muffler-mpn-460031.html) — grade C. MBRP's diesel muffler is a straight-through "spiral louver" core packed with stainless-steel wool and wrapped in a ceramic-fibre blanket; universal **4" Quiet Tone = 30" overall**. [xtremediesel.com](https://www.xtremediesel.com/mbrp-universal-4-quiet-tone-muffler) — grade C. Kits ship **with** a high-flow muffler; straight-pipe/race "muffler delete" variants are also sold.
- **NOT PUBLICLY AVAILABLE:** production-piece weights, exact bend coordinates/lengths, fitted tip diameter (4–6" tips sold separately), any dimensioned drawing or wall-thickness statement from the manufacturer. **Verdict: PARTIALLY PUBLISHED.**

## 5. Low-compression forged pistons, 16.5:1 (`pistons_lowcomp`)

- **Stock compression ratio for the LLY: 17.5:1** (DMAX Store spec table: LLY 17.5:1, 4.055" bore / 3.897" stroke, 310 hp / 605 lb-ft; greenhandlemedia LLY primer: 17.5:1, cast-aluminum pistons). [dmaxstore.com](https://dmaxstore.com/pages/6-6l-duramax-diesel-engine-specs-lb7-lly-lbz-lmm-lml-l5p), [greenhandlemedia.com](https://greenhandlemedia.com/2023/04/2004-5-2006-lly-duramax/) — **grade B/C**. (LBZ/LMM = 16.8:1; do not borrow their values for the LLY.)
- **Published CR options sold for the LLY family (this is the direct answer):**
  - **16.5:1 — Mahle 930029875** ("4.075in Bore 3.898in Stk 6.417in Rod **1.358 Pin -41.8cc 16.5 CR** Set of 8"). [fidanza.com](https://fidanza.com/mahle-ms-piston-set-duramax-4-075in-bore-3-898in-stk-6-417in-rod-1-358-pin-41-8cc-16-5-cr-set-of-8/) — grade C. Also **CP-Carrillo DHR7030 hybrid kit**: "piston has a 4.075" bore and **16.5:1** CR", with a **0.202" shorter CH** and **1.156" pin** (kit pairs the piston with a 0.202" longer rod). [cp-carrillo.com](https://www.cp-carrillo.com/n-12064-http-www-cp-carrillo-com-n-12064-diesel-hybrid-kits-now-availa.html) — grade C.
  - **16.6:1 / 16.7:1 — Mahle 930036055** cast-performance set: 4.055" bore, **CH 1.951"** (a 16.7 variant is listed at 1.956"), pin 1.358", **crown −40.7 cc**, 864 g (850 g for the 16.7 row), **alloy M142**, **Grafal coating**, "CH reduced 0.010"". [lmperformance.com](https://www.lmperformance.com/1258318/930036055-mahle-ms-piston-set-duramax-4055in-bore-3898in-stk-6417in-rod-1358-pin-407cc-167-cr-set-of-8.html) — grade C.
  - **17.0:1 — Mahle 930036095T** (4.095" bore = +0.040", **crown −40.7 cc**, 17.0 CR). [lmperformance.com](https://www.lmperformance.com/1674426/930036095t-mahle-ms-piston-set-gm-duramax-66l-4095in-bore-3898in-stk-6417in-rod-407cc-170-cr-set-of-8.html) — grade C.
  - 16.8:1 cut/coated one-offs appear only in a forum weight/CR post — **grade D, not a catalog option** ([duramaxdiesels.com thread](https://www.duramaxdiesels.com/forum/threads/duramax-monotherm-pistons.35491/page-2)).
- **Bowl/clearance volume:** Mahle publishes **−40.7 cc** (16.6/17.0) and **−41.8 cc** (16.5) crown volumes; L7 §8 has the stock OE-replacement bowl at **+38 cc** (UEM). Sign/reference conventions differ by maker — do not subtract them across makers.
- **DERIVED check of the ratio difference (as the brief anticipates):** with published bore **103 mm** and stroke **99 mm**, swept volume = **825.0 cc/cylinder** (π/4 × 103² × 99); CR 17.5 → clearance volume ≈ **50.0 cc**; CR 16.5 → **53.2 cc**; so 17.5→16.5 needs ≈ **+3.2 cc** of clearance volume. This is arithmetic from published bore/stroke, **not a published bowl figure**.
- **Material / coatings / reliefs (published):** Mahle **M142 cast alloy**, **Grafal** skirt coating, **hard-anodized top ring groove** on the forged race line, "**0.075" deep valve pockets**" variants; Mahle states its CR is calculated with **0.012" piston protrusion above deck and a 0.037" × 4.115" head gasket**. Diamond Pistons: **billet 2618**, **type-3 hard anodizing**, optional **PTFE**, double skirt coating, H13/DLC wrist pins (family-wide page; LLY-specific CR not published there). [mahle.com applications snippet](https://www.mahle.com/en/search/?q=+930036055), [diamondracing.net](https://www.diamondracing.net/diesel-pistons/) — grade C.
- **Valve reliefs:** published only as a pocket depth ("0.075 in") on race pistons; pocket layout, pocket diameter, and bowl depth are **NOT PUBLICLY AVAILABLE**. Piston weight for the 16.5:1 forged parts, ring-groove dimensions, and a dimensioned bowl profile are also not published. **A 4032-alloy LLY piston listing was not located** (the 4032/2618 split is generic Wiseco marketing; Diamond's Duramax product is 2618) — treat "4032" as NOT PUBLICLY AVAILABLE for this engine.
- **Verdict: PARTIALLY PUBLISHED** — CR, cc, CH, pin, alloy and coatings are published; the detailed bowl/relief geometry the 3D model most needs is not.

**Bottom line:** the exhaust diameter (4 in = 101.6 mm), the intercooler envelope (Mishimoto/CSF/PPE,
3" in/out, ~2" core, ~37–38" wide), the intake filter envelope (S&B oval filter, Banks 7-1/8" outlet,
4" turbo inlet), the piston compression ratios/bowl cc/CH/pin, and the turbo's 61–68 mm class are
**published and citable**. What is genuinely missing everywhere is *dimensioned geometry*: no vendor
publishes wheel inducer/exducer pairs, bowl profiles, full tube runs, or drawings. The M8 models will
therefore be well-anchored in **overall size, construction and materials**, and remain **illustrative in
detail shape** — which is exactly the provenance split the M8 spec should state.
