# Carbon Duramax — 2005 Chevrolet 6.6L Duramax LLY V8 (3D Engine)

Interactive, mobile-optimized PBR 3D simulation and Augmented Reality (AR) viewer for the **2005 Chevrolet 6.6L Duramax LLY V8 Turbo-Diesel (VIN Code 2)**.

## Enabled Capabilities
- **Live Mechanical Kinematics (`0–3,000 RPM`)**: Real-time rotating 7-pulley green-backed serpentine belt drive, 9-blade cooling fan, 11-blade 62.6mm Garrett GT3788VA billet compressor wheel, and `680 / 1,200 / 1,600 / 3,000 RPM` presets with live VGT Boost, Bosch CP3 Rail, and Oil Pressure telemetry.
- **Exploded Assembly View (`0–100%`) & X-Ray Crank Cutaway**: Separate all 12 modular OEM subsystems or toggle X-Ray mode to inspect the internal 90° V8 forged crankshaft, connecting rods, and 8 reciprocating pistons.
- **Tap-to-Inspect 12 Subsystems & 6 Camera Presets**: Inspect OEM part numbers and architecture (`GM #21994000` intake resonator, `GM #97382952` fuel filter & priming head, `ACDelco PF2232` oil filter, `ACDelco A1618C` air filter, external Bosch solenoid injectors, and coolant-cooled EGR cooler).
- **Mobile AR & `.GLB` Support**: Built-in `<model-viewer>` Mobile AR mode (iOS Safari Quick Look & Android Chrome Scene Viewer) loading [`Duramax_LLY_2005_66L_V8.glb`](Duramax_LLY_2005_66L_V8.glb), plus one-tap `.GLB` download and auto-scaling 60 FPS mobile PBR performance mode.
- **Synthesized 90° V8 Diesel & VGT Whistle Audio**: Web Audio synthesizer synced to live engine RPM.
